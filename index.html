A SMALL, CAREFUL SERVICE

From a folder of scans to something usable.

01 / YOU SEND

Three readable pages first

Authorized JPG, PNG, or one-page PDF scans of English handwritten family letters, around 1900–1970. One written side is one page, not one letter.

Up to about 350 English words / 35 source lines per qualified page. We check clarity, page order, rights, and our ability to read the writing before quoting.

02 / WE CHECK

AI draft. Human eyes.

A disclosed AI processor makes a first draft only after your consent. A human then compares every line with the original and checks important names, places, and dates again.

We mark uncertain or unreadable words rather than confidently filling in gaps. This is a reviewed transcription, not a guarantee that every word can be recovered.

03 / YOU KEEP

One offline ZIP

  • Unchanged original scans
  • Numbered line-by-line TXT + CSV
  • Side-by-side original/text PDFs
  • Local HTML search with source-line links
  • Uncertain-words list and version notes

No account, subscription, or remote search service. Extract the ZIP and open it in your agreed desktop browser.

A DELIBERATELY NARROW SCOPE

Some letters need a different kind of help.

We do not accept non-English letters, severe fading, heavy show-through, cross-writing, or chaotic mixed handwriting that we cannot confidently follow. Materials outside roughly 1900–1970 are outside this trial.

No translation, paleography, genealogical research, inferred family relationships, automatic family stories, paper scanning, or legal / evidentiary certification. We do not promise all words are recoverable.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your letters are not our marketing.
